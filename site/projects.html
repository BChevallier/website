Jiggling Atoms

Thermodynamics is just statistics in a trenchcoat. Incredibly simple gas models (written in Python, so yes, really simple) yield surprisingly realistic behaviour, and they are great for building intuition. Entropy, osmosis, thermal expansion and why blowing on soup makes it cool faster all become intuitive when thinking of jiggling balls.
