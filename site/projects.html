Jiggling Atoms

Thermodynamics is just statistics in a trenchcoat. Incredibly simple gas models (written in Python, so yes, really simple) yield surprisingly realistic behaviour, and they are great for building intuition.

Here is what this project helped me understand at an intuitive level. Maybe it helps you too: entropy, pressure, temperature, diffusion, osmosis, thermal expansion, the temperature dependence of Young's modulus, gas models, and the Boltzmann and Maxwell distributions.
