What makes a fish a fish?

  • Historically animal phyla (mammals, reptiles, birds, etc.) were defined by convention and common traits.
  • Biologists later started classifying animals by common ancestry.
  • Animals we call "fish" are very far apart evolutionarily.
  • A common ancestor would also include all mammals. That's a problem.
The common ancestor of salmons and sharks lived roughly ~450 Million years ago. Salmons are more closely related to humans than to sharks.

Let's ask Wikipedia.

A fish is an aquatic, anamniotic, gill-bearing vertebrate animal with a tough cranium to protect the brain , but lacking limbs with digits .
